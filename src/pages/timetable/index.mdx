students actually turn up with is not what the course is about at all. It is
whether they can fit the thing around a Wednesday shift, and that is a
perfectly reasonable question to want answered before you enrol.

:::info[The shape of a week]
You should budget for two contact hours a week, plus roughly a third hour that
you spend outside on your own queue in your own time. The Monday lecture runs
for an hour and it is an argument rather than a worked example, so do come
ready to disagree. The Wednesday field session is two hours long and most of
them are not held in a room at all.
:::

## What the dates commit to

None of the dates on this page are typed out by hand, which is deliberate. The
table is generated from the lecture, session and assessment pages themselves,
so a date can only be wrong here if it is also wrong there, and `spec/course-shape.test.ts` fails the build if the
twelve weeks stop lining up with the course record.

That is not a small thing on a course website. A timetable is the page students
plan a semester against, and a stale one is worse than no timetable at all,
because it is believed.

## Reading the load

| Load | Weeks | What it costs you |
|---|---|---|
| Light | 1, 2, 12 | Choosing a queue, one clipboard exercise, the defence |
| Steady | 3, 4, 8, 9, 10 | A lecture, a field session, one journal entry |
| Heavy | 5, 6, 7, 11 | The above plus an assessment inside two weeks |

The two genuinely busy stretches are weeks 4 to 5, when The Measured Queue
lands on top of the pooling material, and weeks 10 to 12, when the journal, the
studio and the defence arrive together. Neither is an accident and both are
survivable, but plan the second one before week 9 rather than during it.

:::warning[Week 10 comes with a warning]
The week 10 lecture works from published inquiry reports, several of which
concern deaths in crowd failures. There is a written version that carries the
same material, and the [policies page](/policies/) says how to take it. You do
not have to give a reason.
:::

## If you miss a week

:::details[What to do, in order]
Field sessions are where the data comes from, so a missed one costs you more
than a missed lecture. In order of what to do about it.

1. Tell Priya the same week. Most sessions can be repeated with another group
   or done alone with a sheet she will send you.
2. Get the week's measurements anyway, even roughly. An entry in the journal
   saying you collected thirty arrivals instead of sixty is worth far more than
   a gap.
3. If the site itself has fallen through rather than your attendance, that is a
   different problem and the [policies page](/policies/) covers it. It is not a
   penalty and it happens most years.

Lectures are recorded, with one exception. Week 7 is a guest lecture and it is
not recorded at the guest's request.
:::
