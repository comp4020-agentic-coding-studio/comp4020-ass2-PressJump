Four people teach this course and each entry says which weeks, so you can tell
who to ask about what.

Course-level questions go to Tessa. Anything about the arithmetic goes to Amos.
Anything about a site, a permission, a stopwatch or a queue that has stopped
existing goes to Priya, and it goes to her first rather than after you have
spent a fortnight trying to solve it alone.

## Consultation

Every profile gives its own hours and how to reach that person. Between them
there is staffed time on Tuesday, Thursday and Wednesday afternoons, which
covers most of the week.

The convenor's consultation runs as a walk-in rather than a booking sheet,
because the questions that matter most in this course are usually the ones a
student had not planned to ask. If you need a guaranteed slot, email and one
will be arranged.
