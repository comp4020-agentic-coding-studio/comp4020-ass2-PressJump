Four people teach this course and each entry says which weeks, so you can tell
who to ask about what.

Course-level questions go to Tessa. Anything about the arithmetic goes to Amos.
Anything about a site, a permission, a stopwatch or a queue that has stopped
existing goes to Priya, and it goes to her first rather than after you have
spent a fortnight trying to solve it alone.

<PeopleGrid />

## What each profile carries

Office, phone, consultation hours, what to ask that person about, what they
work on, and the weeks they teach. The teaching list on each profile is built
from the lecture and session pages themselves rather than typed in, so it
cannot drift out of step with the timetable.

## Consultation, honestly

The convenor's consultation is a first-come-first-served walk-in queue with one
server and no published wait. It is the worst-designed queue any of us interact
with weekly and it is left that way on purpose, because by week 5 you can say
exactly what is wrong with it and by week 11 you have a fix.

Students have proposed four over the years. Two were adopted, one made things
worse in a way nobody predicted, and one is written up in the week 11 lecture
notes as the best wrong answer this course has produced.
