Four of us teach this course, and each entry below says which weeks that
person takes, so you can work out fairly quickly who to ask about what.

As a rough guide, course-level questions are best taken to Tessa, and anything
to do with the arithmetic is Amos. If it is about a site, a permission, a
stopwatch, or a queue that has inconveniently stopped existing, then Priya is
the one to ask. Please do go to her early rather than after you have spent a
fortnight trying to sort it out on your own, because that happens every year
and it never helps anybody.

## Consultation

Every profile lists its own hours and the best way to reach that person.
Between the four of us there is staffed time on Tuesday, Thursday and Wednesday
afternoons, which covers most of the week reasonably well.

The convenor's consultation runs as a walk-in rather than a booking sheet, and
that is a deliberate choice. The questions that matter most in this course tend
to be the ones a student had not planned on asking, and a booking sheet quietly
filters those out. If you would rather have a guaranteed slot, just email and
one will be arranged.
