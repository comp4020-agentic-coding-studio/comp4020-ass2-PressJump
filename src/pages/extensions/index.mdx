Ask before the deadline if you can. Up to five days is granted by the convenor
by email, the reason does not have to be documented, and you will get an answer
the same day.

:::tip[The short version]
"Something has come up and I need until Wednesday" is a complete request. You
do not have to explain a medical or personal matter to anybody in this course,
and you do not have to attach anything.
:::

## Three things worth knowing before you ask

**Asking early is free and asking late is not.** An extension agreed before the
deadline costs you nothing. The same request sent two days after it has already
started accruing a penalty on most pieces, and on one piece it is too late
entirely.

**A third request in one semester is still usually yes.** Tessa will also ask
whether the course is the problem, which is a real question rather than a
warning, and the answer has changed the course twice.

**More than five days is a different process.** The convenor cannot grant it by
email. Send the request anyway and you will be pointed at the university's
standard form rather than left to find it.

## What happens after you submit

You get a decision the same day, usually within a couple of hours during the
working week. If it is a yes, the new date is in the reply and the reply is the
record, so keep it.

If nothing has arrived within one working day, submit it again rather than
waiting. An unanswered request is an invisible queue of exactly the kind week 7
is about, and the person at the front of one cannot tell whether it is moving.

The full rules, including what late work costs and how access adjustments work,
are on the [policies page](/policies/).
