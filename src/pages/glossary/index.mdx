There are twenty-three terms here, and you will meet them in roughly the order
they are listed. Each one records the week it turns up in, so if a word on a
lecture page is quietly doing work you cannot follow, the explanation should be
here and it will be dated.

:::tip[Three that trip everybody]
**Balking and reneging** are different and both wreck an arrival count.
**Utilisation** is not efficiency, it is the thing that explodes near one. And
**starvation** is a technical word for a specific failure, not a synonym for a
long wait.
:::

## What is deliberately not here

You will not find an entry for anything you could work out from the page in
ten seconds, and there is nothing here for a term the course only uses once. A
glossary that lists absolutely everything tends to be a glossary nobody opens
twice.

There is also nothing here on queueing theory's standard notation, meaning the
A/B/C strings like M/M/1 and M/M/c. They only appear twice in week 4 and once
in week 9, they are explained on the spot each time, and learning to read them
fluently is not something this course is going to ask of you.
