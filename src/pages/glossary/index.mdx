Twenty-three terms. You meet them in this order and each one says which week it
arrives in, so if something in a lecture page is doing work you cannot follow,
the answer is here and it is dated.

:::tip[Three that trip everybody]
**Balking and reneging** are different and both wreck an arrival count.
**Utilisation** is not efficiency, it is the thing that explodes near one. And
**starvation** is a technical word for a specific failure, not a synonym for a
long wait.
:::

## What is deliberately not here

No entry for anything you can read off a page in ten seconds, and no entry for
a term the course uses once. A glossary that lists everything is a glossary
nobody opens twice.

There is also no entry for queueing theory's standard notation, the
A/B/C strings like M/M/1 and M/M/c. They appear twice in week 4 and once in
week 9, they are explained where they appear, and learning to read them is not
something this course asks of you.
