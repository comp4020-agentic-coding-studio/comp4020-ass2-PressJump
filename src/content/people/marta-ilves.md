I processed applications for eleven years and then spent four more
investigating the offices that process them. A visible queue is a courtesy. It
tells you how many people are ahead of you and roughly what that is worth in
minutes. Most of the queues that decide anything important about a person's
life offer neither.

I come in for one week to talk about what happens to a queue when nobody can
see its length, including what happens to the people running it.
