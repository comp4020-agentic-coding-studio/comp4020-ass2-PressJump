I spent nine years as a flow manager at a large public hospital. The job was
described to me as logistics. It was not. It was deciding, several hundred
times a shift, whose afternoon was worth less than somebody else's, and then
writing that decision down as a number so that nobody had to say it out loud.

That is the course. Queueing theory gives you the arithmetic, and the
arithmetic is genuinely useful, but the equations are silent on the only
question that matters at the counter. I teach weeks 1, 5, 6, 11 and 12.
