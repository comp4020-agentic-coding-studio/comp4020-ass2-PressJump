I plan crowd movement for stations and stadiums, which is the same job as
planning a queue except that the consequences of being wrong are worse. I run
most of the field sessions, which means I am the person who will tell you that
your sample of eleven arrivals is not a sample.

Two things I will say to nearly everyone at some point in the semester.
Measure the thing you can actually measure, not the thing you wish you could.
And go back a second time, on a different day, before you believe anything.

## What to ask me about

Anything to do with a site. Permission, access, a manager who has said no, a
shop that has closed, weather, safety, or a queue that turns out to be four
queues. Ask in week 1 rather than week 6; almost everything is fixable early
and almost nothing is fixable late.

Also the clipboard. People are embarrassed to ask how to hold it. Ask.

## Consultation

I am in the field sessions and for half an hour afterwards. That half hour is
the most useful thirty minutes in the course and almost nobody uses it.
