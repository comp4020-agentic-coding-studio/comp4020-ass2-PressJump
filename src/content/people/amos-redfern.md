I came to queues through operating systems, which is a strange door to come
through and a useful one. A CPU scheduler and a passport office solve the same
problem, and the operating system has the better documentation.

My half of the course is the part students expect to be the hard part. It is
not. There are four pieces of arithmetic in twelve weeks and you have met
three of them before. What makes them hard is that they keep telling you
things about the world you would rather not know, such as the fact that a
counter running at 95 percent capacity is not a well-run counter.

I teach weeks 2, 3, 4, 9 and 10.
