Six weeks into the bakery counter and the assistant asked me today to stop
standing near the till. Politely, and I stopped immediately, but that is my
site gone.

I have observation sheets from weeks 2, 3 and 5, the histogram, and about
ninety service times. What I do not have is anywhere to trial the redesign.

Do I start again somewhere else, or is there a version of this where the data
I already have is enough?
