Week 6 is the last teaching week before the break. Week 7 resumes on 19 April.

Two things before you disappear.

Keep counting. You do not need a full session, and two twenty minute visits
across the break are worth more than the three weeks of nothing most people
hand in. If your site is a campus queue that closes, say so in the journal and
pick a second site for the gap.

Bring your raw sheets back. Not the spreadsheet, the paper. Week 7 opens with
you swapping sheets with somebody else and finding what they recorded that you
did not.
