Counted the till for an hour. Arrivals per minute went 1.2, then 3.8, then
2.1, then 6.4 when a bus emptied out.

If I average those I get 3.4, which describes no ten minutes of that hour. If I
use the busy one I get a wait that most people never had.

Which number is the one the formula wants?
