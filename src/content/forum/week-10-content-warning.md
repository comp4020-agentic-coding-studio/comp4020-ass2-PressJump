Posting in case it helps somebody deciding this week.

I took the written version of the week 10 lecture. It is the same material,
it is about the engineering rather than about the people, and it took me
about half an hour to read. Nobody asked me why, and the post-mortem session
on Wednesday had two reports without fatalities in the pile so I did not have
to ask for that either.

The thing I had wrong was assuming the written version would be a summary. It
is not, it is the lecture written down, including the part about why density
is the quantity that tells you where the boundary is.
