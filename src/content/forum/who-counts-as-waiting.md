Standing at the bank counter on Tuesday. Eleven people in line, eight of them
on phones, and none of them looked annoyed.

The person at the front with no phone looked furious.

If waiting is a cost, these people are not paying the same one, and my
stopwatch cannot tell the difference. Does the course have a way to record
that, or is it just seconds and nothing else?
