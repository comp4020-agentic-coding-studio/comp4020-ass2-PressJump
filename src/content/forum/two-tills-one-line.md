Counted 14 in the line, arrivals about 3 a minute, wait around 4 minutes and 40
seconds. That matched the stopwatch, which was satisfying.

Then they opened a second till onto the same line. Same arrivals. Line length
dropped to 5 and my measured wait dropped to about 1 minute 20.

I expected half. I got a quarter. Have I made an arithmetic mistake or is this a
real thing?
