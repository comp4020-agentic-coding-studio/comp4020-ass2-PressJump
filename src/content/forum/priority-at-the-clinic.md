Been counting the walk in clinic for three weeks. Arrival order and service
order are not the same thing and I cannot work out what decides it.

Watched a man wait fifty minutes while four people who arrived after him went
through. He asked twice. Both times he was told it would not be long.

I think there is a triage rule and I think it is probably a good one. Nothing at
that counter says so, and from a chair in that room it reads as the staff
deciding they like some people more than others.
