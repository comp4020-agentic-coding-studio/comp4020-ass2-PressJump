The pharmacy counter I picked in week 1 has one line. I have been treating it
as one queue for two weeks.

Standing there on Saturday I realised it is serving four different things from
that line. Prescription drop-off is about forty seconds. Collection is about
twenty. A consultation is four to six minutes and happens maybe once every
fifteen customers. Retail is a normal shop transaction.

So my "mean service time" is an average across four completely different jobs,
and it is not a number anybody in that queue has ever experienced.

Do I split it into four? Do I pick one type and ignore the rest? Or do I need a
different site?
