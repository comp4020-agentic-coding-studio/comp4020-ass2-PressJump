      Start with bins about a quarter of the median service time and look at what
      happens. You are hunting for a shape, and the shape you care about here is
      whether there is one hump or two. If both bin widths show two humps, the two
      humps are real. If only one width shows them, they are an artefact and you say
      so in the journal.
