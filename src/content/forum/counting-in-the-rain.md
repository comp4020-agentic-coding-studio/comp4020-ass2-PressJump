Thursday session at the coffee cart. Started dry with eleven people in line.

It started raining properly about fifteen minutes in. Within five minutes the
line was three people and stayed there. Arrivals did not stop, people looked at
the line and kept walking.

So my arrival rate for that window is not the demand, it is the demand minus
everybody who decided it was not worth standing in the wet.

Is that session ruined, or is it the most interesting forty minutes I have
recorded?
