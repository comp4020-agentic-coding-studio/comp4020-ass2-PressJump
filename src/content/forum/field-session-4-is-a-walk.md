Wednesday's session is on foot. We visit four queues, ten minutes of looking
and ten minutes of arguing at each, and we finish at the bus interchange
rather than back in the building.

**Bring** shoes you can walk forty minutes in, a notebook, and your five
reasons not to pool written down. No stopwatch needed; this one is about
looking.

**Weather.** We walk in anything short of a storm. If it is cancelled you will
know by 09:00 on the day, here and by email.

**If walking forty minutes is not workable for you**, tell me before Tuesday
and there is a seated version that uses the same four queues from a different
angle. It is routine, it takes one email, and nobody else needs to know.
