Read this before Wednesday. The queue you choose in the week 1 field session
is the queue in three of the four assessments, and changing it after week 2
means starting the measurement again.

Four things make a queue workable here.

- You can get to it twice in a week without it being a trip.
- At least thirty arrivals an hour in its busy period.
- You can see the joining point and the service point from one position.
- Somebody is in charge of it and could in principle be asked about it.

Four ways a first choice usually fails. It only exists at 08:00 on a Tuesday.
You cannot legally stand next to it. It has three customers an hour. Or it is
not one queue at all, it is four, and you will not notice until week 3.

Bring a second choice. About a third of first choices do not survive Wednesday
and that is the session working, not you failing.
