The week 8 session came with a whole consent procedure for asking three people
a question. Say who you are, accept a no immediately, do not approach anybody
distressed.

Which made me notice that for the other eleven weeks I have been recording
roughly two hundred people a session without any of them knowing, and nobody
has asked me to say anything to anybody.

I am not saying the policy is wrong. I am saying I cannot articulate why one
of those needs a script and the other does not, and I would like to be able
to.
